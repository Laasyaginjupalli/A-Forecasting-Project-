# Sales Forecasting Analysis – NovaTech Electronics

## Project Overview

This project focuses on analysing and forecasting monthly sales data for NovaTech Electronics using eight years of historical sales information. The analysis applies time-series forecasting techniques to identify sales trends, seasonal patterns, and future demand. Additive and multiplicative seasonality models were developed and compared using Prophet to identify the most suitable forecasting approach. The findings demonstrate how predictive analytics can support sales planning, inventory management, and data-driven business decisions.

## Objectives

- Analyse historical monthly sales data to identify trends and seasonal variations.
- Develop time-series forecasting models using additive and multiplicative seasonality.
- Compare model performance based on forecasting error metrics.
- Identify monthly seasonal effects and changes in sales demand.
- Generate future sales forecasts with confidence intervals.
- Evaluate how forecasting insights can support inventory and business planning.
- Apply quantitative forecasting to improve data-driven decision-making.

## Key Findings

- **Model Comparison:** The multiplicative seasonality model demonstrated lower forecasting errors compared to the additive model, making it a more suitable fit for the dataset.
- **Seasonal Patterns:** January recorded a positive seasonal effect of approximately 11%, indicating sales were around 11% above the average seasonal level.
- **Sales Trends:** Sales gradually declined from April, reaching their lowest seasonal level around September, before increasing from October.
- **Forecast Accuracy:** The multiplicative model captured the increasing sales trend and seasonal fluctuations more effectively.
- **Business Insights:** Forecasting results can help NovaTech Electronics anticipate high-demand periods, plan promotional activities, optimise inventory levels, and minimise overstocking and understocking risks.
